Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

How to Delete Your Data

Holy Week stores game progress locally on your device and, if you are signed in, in our cloud database. You can delete your data at any time using the methods below.

Quick Delete (Local Data)

To immediately delete all locally stored game progress, open the game and tap the Reset button in the bottom navigation bar. This will clear all case progress, scores, and accessibility settings from your device.

Delete Account & Cloud Data

If you have signed in with Google and have cloud-synced progress, you can request full account deletion by emailing us at privacy@pixelagent.co.uk with the subject line "Delete My Holy Week Account".

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • A brief request to delete your account and all associated data

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm once your data has been permanently deleted from our servers.

Data Deletion Callback

Meta requires that apps provide a data deletion callback endpoint. This endpoint allows Meta to trigger account deletion on behalf of users.

Data Deletion Callback URL:

https://pixelagent.co.uk/holy-week/data-deletion

Status: Endpoint placeholder — implementation pending server-side processing

When a user requests deletion through Meta, a POST request will be sent to this endpoint with a user identifier. Our server will then:

  • Delete all user data from Firestore
  • Clear any cached data
  • Return a confirmation response to Meta

Note: The callback endpoint is currently a placeholder. For immediate data deletion, please use the in-app Reset button or email us directly at privacy@pixelagent.co.uk.

What Data We Delete

When you request deletion, we remove:

  • Game progress (completed cases, scores, evidence collected)
  • Accessibility preferences
  • Any cloud-synced account information
  • All associated records from our database

Retention Policy

Deleted data cannot be recovered. We retain backup copies for a maximum of 30 days before permanent erasure, as required by our infrastructure provider.

For any questions about data deletion or privacy, please contact us at privacy@pixelagent.co.uk.
