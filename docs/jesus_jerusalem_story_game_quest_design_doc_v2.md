# Story Game Design – The Final Week in Jerusalem
## 12-Phase Systemic Chronology (Extended Archive Architecture)

## Game Premise
The player functions as an investigative teenage apprentice scribe tracking shifting civil parameters, legal inconsistencies, and radical social phenomena across historic Jerusalem's final week.

---

# Main Quest Structure by Linear Phase

## 🌴 ACT 1: THE POPULIST ARRIVAL

### Phase 1: The Donkey King
- **Focus:** Tracking regional messianic expectations, localized palm branch processions, and Roman tower observation metrics.
- **Board Tokens:** `Prophecy Fulfilled`, `Populist Entrance Vector`.

### Phase 2: Rumours in the Marketplace
- **Focus:** Basic introduction to dialogue value bartering, street intelligence gathering, and marketplace patrol routing.
- **Board Tokens:** `Market Ledger Details`, `Gossip Commodity Key`.

---

## 🏛️ ACT 2: THE ANOMALIES

### Phase 3: The Man Born Blind
- **Focus:** Tracking unexpected Bethesda pool anomalies, recording Pharisaic forensic interrogation methods, and navigating localized legal pressure.
- **Board Tokens:** `Bethesda Eyewitness Account`, `Sabbath Compliance Objection`.

### Phase 4: The Cleansed Temple
- **Focus:** Analyzing acute economic disruption, structural priestly cash-flow anomalies, and public unrest metrics.
- **Board Tokens:** `Overturned Accounting Ledger`, `Auxiliary Garrison Assessment`.

### Phase 5: Whispers About Lazarus
- **Focus:** Documenting extreme theological escalation parameters, tracking secret movements outside city lines, and measuring high-society counter-espionage alerts.
- **Board Tokens:** `Mortality Subversion Report`, `Sanhedrin Surveillance Mandate`.

---

## 🍷 ACT 3: THE MOUNTING PRESSURE

### Phase 6: Dinner in Bethany
- **Focus:** Auditing core disciple group resource scarcity, domestic defense logistics, and internal circle mental exhaustion variables.
- **Board Tokens:** `Bethany Anointing Incident`, `Disciple Resource Deficit`.

### Phase 7: The Teacher Speaks
- **Focus:** Resolving public ideological debates in the outer courts, countering elite binary arguments, and recording institutional panic updates.
- **Board Tokens:** `Authority Contravention`, `Sadducean Absolute Policy`.

### Phase 8: Stories With Hidden Meanings
- **Focus:** Processing allegorical code matrices, translating vineyard subversion parables, and extracting geopolitical subtext.
- **Board Tokens:** `Rebellious Tenant Dossier`, `Rejected Cornerstone Formula`.

---

## 🌌 ACT 4: THE CONDEMNATION MACHINE

### Phase 9: The Last Supper
- **Focus:** Identifying internal counter-intelligence networks, managing betrayal detection parameters, and unpacking cryptic ceremonial syntax.
- **Board Tokens:** `Upper Room Protocol`, `Asymmetrical Information Cash`.

### Phase 10: The Garden & Sanhedrin
- **Focus:** Recording Gethsemane capture tracking matrices, late-night high-priesthood judicial tribunals, and conflicting statement logic.
- **Board Tokens:** `Midnight Arrest Log`, `Tribunal Counter-Contradictions`.

### Phase 11: The Imperial Legality
- **Focus:** Translating blasphemy claims into Roman sedition files, tracking the 'King of the Jews' defense loop, and managing mob psychology during the Barabbas swap.
- **Board Tokens:** `Imperial Bureaucracy Brief`, `Sedition Formula Titulus`, `Mob Variable Weight`.

---

## 🪦 ACT 5: THE FINAL SUMMARY & CONTINUATION

### Phase 12: Deterrence, Secret Loyalty & Resurrection
- **Focus:** Chronicling crucifixion deterrence execution routines, processing apocalyptic climate phenomena, tracking post-execution group defense trees, managing burial security parameters, and investigating early tomb subversion rumors.
- **Board Tokens:** `Gallows Soldier Assessment`, `Veil Rupture Metric`, `Peter Restoration Dialogue`, `Sealed Crypt Protocol`, `Resurrection Inconsistency Logs`.
