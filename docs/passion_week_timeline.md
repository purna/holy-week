# Timeline of Passion Week: From the Triumphal Entry to the Resurrection

A comprehensive, chronological breakdown of the historic events of Jesus Christ's final week, including fulfillment of Old Testament prophecies, precise locations, estimated times, and verified witnesses.

---

## 1. The Triumphal Entry into Jerusalem riding on a don
- **Day:** Sunday (Palm Sunday)
- **Approximate Time:** Afternoon
- **Location:** Mount of Olives / Jerusalem
- **Event Description:** The Triumphal Entry into Jerusalem riding on a donkey.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 21:1–11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+21%3A1-11&version=NIV), [Mark 11:1–11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+11%3A1-11&version=NIV), [Luke 19:28–44](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+19%3A28-44&version=NIV), [John 12:12–19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+12%3A12-19&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** The Messiah would enter Jerusalem riding on a donkey, bringing salvation and peace.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Zechariah 9:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Zechariah+9%3A9&version=NIV), [Psalm 118:25-26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+118%3A25-26&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** The Disciples, the crowds of Judean pilgrims, Pharisees, and residents of Jerusalem.

- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 69:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+69%3A9&version=NIV), [Isaiah 56:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+56%3A7&version=NIV), [Malachi 3:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Malachi+3%3A1&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** The Disciples, Temple merchants, money changers, Chief Priests, Teachers of the Law, and the crowds.

## 6. The Olivet Discourse
- **Day:** Tuesday
- **Approximate Time:** Late Afternoon / Evening
- **Location:** Mount of Olives
- **Event Description:** The Olivet Discourse: Jesus prophecies the destruction of the Temple and signs of the End of the Age.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 24:1–25:46](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+24%3A1-25%3A46&version=NIV), [Mark 13:1–37](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+13%3A1-37&version=NIV), [Luke 21:5–36](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+21%3A5-36&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** The destruction of the city and temple, and abomination of desolation.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Daniel 9:26-27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Daniel+9%3A26-27&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Peter, James, John, and Andrew (privately).

## 7. Judas Iscariot conspires with the Sanhedrin to bet
- **Day:** Wednesday (Spy Wednesday)
- **Approximate Time:** Daytime
- **Location:** Jerusalem / Palace of the High Priest
- **Event Description:** Judas Iscariot conspires with the Sanhedrin to betray Jesus for thirty pieces of silver.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 26:14–16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+26%3A14-16&version=NIV), [Mark 14:10–11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+14%3A10-11&version=NIV), [Luke 22:3–6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+22%3A3-6&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** Betrayed by a close friend for exactly thirty pieces of silver, which are later thrown into the house of the Lord for the potter.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 41:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+41%3A9&version=NIV), [Zechariah 11:12-13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Zechariah+11%3A12-13&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Judas Iscariot, Chief Priests, and officers of the Temple guard.

- **Day:** Thursday (Maundy Thursday)
- **Approximate Time:** Evening (Sunset)
- **Location:** The Upper Room, Jerusalem
- **Event Description:** The Last Supper: Jesus washes the disciples' feet, institutes the Lord's Supper, identifies His betrayer, and gives the Upper Room Discourse.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 26:17–30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+26%3A17-30&version=NIV), [Mark 14:12–26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+14%3A12-26&version=NIV), [Luke 22:7–30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+22%3A7-30&version=NIV), [John 13:1–17:26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+13%3A1-17%3A26&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** A close friend sharing bread lifts his heel against Him.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 41:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+41%3A9&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** The Twelve Disciples (including Judas Iscariot initially).

## 10. Betrayal and Arrest
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Early Morning (Approx. 1:00 AM – 3:00 AM)
- **Location:** Garden of Gethsemane
- **Event Description:** Betrayal and Arrest: Judas leads an armed crowd; Jesus is arrested; Peter cuts off Malchus's ear and Jesus heals it; the disciples flee.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 26:47–56](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+26%3A47-56&version=NIV), [Mark 14:43–52](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+14%3A43-52&version=NIV), [Luke 22:47–53](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+22%3A47-53&version=NIV), [John 12:1–11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+18%3A1-11&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** The shepherd is struck and the sheep scatter.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Zechariah 13:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Zechariah+13%3A7&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** The Disciples, Judas Iscariot, Roman soldiers, Temple guards, Chief Priests, and Elders.

## 11. Religious Trials
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Early Morning (Approx. 2:00 AM – 5:00 AM)
- **Location:** Palace of Annas & Caiaphas (High Priests)
- **Event Description:** Religious Trials: Jesus is interrogated by Annas, then tried before Caiaphas and the Sanhedrin. False witnesses testify. Jesus is mocked and beaten. Simultaneously, Peter denies Jesus three times before the rooster crows.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 26:57–75](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+26%3A57-75&version=NIV), [Mark 14:53–72](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+14%3A53-72&version=NIV), [Luke 22:54–65](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+22%3A54-65&version=NIV), [John 18:12–27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+18%3A12-27&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** False witnesses rise against Him; He gives His back to those who beat Him and His cheeks to those who pull out His beard; He does not hide His face from mocking and spitting.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 27:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+27%3A12&version=NIV), [Psalm 35:11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+35%3A11&version=NIV), [Isaiah 50:6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+50%3A6&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Caiaphas, Annas, the Sanhedrin, false witnesses, Peter, John (in the courtyard), servants, and guards.

## 12. Civil Trials Part 1
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Dawn (Approx. 6:00 AM)
- **Location:** The Praetorium (Antonia Fortress / Herod's Palace)
- **Event Description:** Civil Trials Part 1: The Sanhedrin officially condemns Jesus and delivers Him to Pontius Pilate, the Roman Governor. Judas hangs himself around this time.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 27:1–14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+27%3A1-14&version=NIV), [Mark 15:1–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+15%3A1-5&version=NIV), [Luke 23:1–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+23%3A1-5&version=NIV), [John 18:28–38](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+18%3A28-38&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** Kings and rulers take counsel together against the Lord and His Anointed. He remains silent before His accusers.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 2:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+2%3A2&version=NIV), [Isaiah 53:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+53%3A7&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Pontius Pilate, Chief Priests, Elders, Roman soldiers, and the gathering crowd.

## 13. Civil Trials Part 2
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Early Morning (Approx. 7:00 AM)
- **Location:** Herod’s Palace, Jerusalem
- **Event Description:** Civil Trials Part 2: Pilate sends Jesus to Herod Antipas (who ruled Galilee). Herod questions Him, mocks Him with an elegant robe, and sends Him back to Pilate.
- **Bible Passages:** [Luke 23:6–12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+23%3A6-12&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** The Messiah is mocked and treated with contempt by rulers.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 22:6-7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+22%3A6-7&version=NIV), [Isaiah 53:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+53%3A3&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Herod Antipas, his soldiers, Chief Priests, Scribes, and attendants.

---

## 14. Civil Trials Part 3
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Morning (Approx. 8:00 AM)
- **Location:** The Praetorium, Jerusalem
- **Event Description:** Civil Trials Part 3: Pilate tries to release Jesus, but the crowd demands Barabbas instead. Pilate washes his hands; Jesus is brutally scourged, crowned with thorns, mocked, and sentenced to crucifixion.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 27:15–31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+27%3A15-31&version=NIV), [Mark 15:6–20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+15%3A6-20&version=NIV), [Luke 23:13–25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+23%3A13-25&version=NIV), [John 18:39–19:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+18%3A39-19%3A16&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** By His stripes (wounds) we are healed. He was despised and rejected.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Isaiah 53:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+53%3A5&version=NIV), [Isaiah 53:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+53%3A3&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Pontius Pilate, Roman soldiers, Chief Priests, Jewish leaders, and the incited Jerusalem crowd.

## 15. The Crucifixion Begins
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Morning (Approx. 9:00 AM)
- **Location:** The Via Dolorosa to Golgotha (Calvary)
- **Event Description:** The Crucifixion Begins: Jesus carries His cross; Simon of Cyrene is forced to help. Jesus is nailed to the cross between two thieves. Soldiers cast lots for His garments.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 27:32–44](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+27%3A32-44&version=NIV), [Mark 15:21–32](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+15%3A21-32&version=NIV), [Luke 23:26–43](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+23%3A26-43&version=NIV), [John 19:17–24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+19%3A17-24&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** Pierced hands and feet; divided garments and cast lots; numbered with the transgressors; offered wine mixed with gall/vinegar.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 22:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+22%3A16&version=NIV), [Psalm 22:18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+22%3A18&version=NIV), [Isaiah 53:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+53%3A12&version=NIV), [Psalm 69:21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+69%3A21&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Roman centurion and soldiers, Simon of Cyrene, the two thieves, Chief Priests, onlookers, and the faithful women (Mary Magdalene, Mary mother of Jesus, John).

## 16. Darkness covers the land. At 3
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Mid-day to Afternoon (12:00 PM – 3:00 PM)
- **Location:** Golgotha (Calvary)
- **Event Description:** Darkness covers the land. At 3:00 PM, Jesus cries out and dies. The Temple veil tears from top to bottom, an earthquake occurs, and graves open.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 27:45–56](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+27%3A45-56&version=NIV), [Mark 15:33–41](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+15%3A33-41&version=NIV), [Luke 23:44–49](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+23%3A44-49&version=NIV), [John 19:25–30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+19%3A25-30&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** The sun goes down at noon; 'My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?'; Commends His spirit; No bones broken; Pierced side.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Amos 8:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Amos+8%3A9&version=NIV), [Psalm 22:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+22%3A1&version=NIV), [Psalm 31:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+31%3A5&version=NIV), [Psalm 34:20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+34%3A20&version=NIV), [Zechariah 12:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Zechariah+12%3A10&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** The Roman Centurion (who confesses 'Surely this was the Son of God'), Roman soldiers, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, Salome, John, and the crowds.

## 17. The Burial
- **Day:** Friday (Good Friday)
- **Approximate Time:** Late Afternoon (Approx. 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM)
- **Location:** A nearby garden tomb owned by Joseph of Arimathea
- **Event Description:** The Burial: Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus request Jesus's body, wrap it in spices and linen, and place it in a new tomb. A large stone is rolled across the entrance.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 27:57–61](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+27%3A57-61&version=NIV), [Mark 15:42–47](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+15%3A42-47&version=NIV), [Luke 23:50–56](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+23%3A50-56&version=NIV), [John 19:38–42](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+19%3A38-42&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** Assigned a grave with the wicked, and with the rich in His death.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Isaiah 53:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+53%3A9&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Joseph of Arimathea, Nicodemus, Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of Joses.

## 19. The Resurrection
- **Day:** Sunday (Resurrection Sunday)
- **Approximate Time:** Early Morning (Dawn / Before Sunrise)
- **Location:** The Tomb of Joseph of Arimathea
- **Event Description:** The Resurrection: An earthquake occurs as an angel rolls away the stone. The guards flee in terror. Jesus rises from the dead, defeating death.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 28:1–4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+28%3A1-4&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** God would not abandon His Holy One to the realm of the dead, nor let His faithful one see decay.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Psalm 16:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+16%3A10&version=NIV), [Hosea 6:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hosea+6%3A2&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** The Roman Guard unit (who felt the earthquake and saw the angel).

## 20. The Empty Tomb and First Appearances
- **Day:** Sunday (Resurrection Sunday)
- **Approximate Time:** Sunrise / Early Morning
- **Location:** The Tomb / Garden
- **Event Description:** The Empty Tomb and First Appearances: Mary Magdalene and other women find the stone rolled away. Angels announce Jesus is alive. Jesus appears to Mary Magdalene and then to the other women.
- **Bible Passages:** [Matthew 28:5–10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+28%3A5-10&version=NIV), [Mark 16:1–11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+16%3A1-11&version=NIV), [Luke 24:1–12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+24%3A1-12&version=NIV), [John 20:1–18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+20%3A1-18&version=NIV)
- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** He will swallow up death forever; Proclaiming good news.
- **Prophecy Reference:** [Isaiah 25:8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+25%3A8&version=NIV), [Isaiah 52:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+52%3A7&version=NIV)
- **Witnesses:** Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, Salome, Peter, and John (who ran to inspect the empty tomb).
