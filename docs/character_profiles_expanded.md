# Character Dialogue Profiles
## Narrative Archetypes & Linguistic Design for Choice-Driven Scripts

This document establishes the comprehensive character profiles for the speakers featured across the 13 dialogue matrices. It maps out their linguistic styles, social hierarchies, psychological pressures, and dialogue strategies to serve as an authoritative reference for narrative design and systemic conversation trees.

---

## 🏛️ Group 1: The Scribes & Observers
### Analytical, High Literacy, Structurally Detached

#### 1. The Senior Scribe / Investigative Mentor
* **Source Dialogues:** `board_debate.json`, `board_review.json`
* **Identity:** An aging, highly literate Judean scholar, archivist, or administrative mentor evaluating an apprentice scribe named Maluch. He serves as an intellectual gatekeeper who separates historical anomalies from theological bias.
* **Social Status:** High institutional and intellectual status. He does not rely on physical or political coercion to command authority; his power lies entirely in sharp editorial critique and epistemic precision.
* **Apparent Age:** Late 50s to mid-60s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Low physical stress, but high systemic anxiety. He recognizes that historical documentation is shifting beneath his feet and warns his student against letting personal affection corrupt objective record-keeping.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Academic, balanced, and deliberately arranged in descriptive triads (*"the witness from the pool, the Roman record from Rome, the accounts from Mark's house"*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Abstract, conceptual, and clinical. He relies on words that categorize reality: *draft, witness, threat, categories, evidence, conclusions*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He acts as a narrative mirror for the player. He does not issue answers; he presents analytical queries (*"Can you tell the difference yet?"*) that challenge the player's integrity or forcing them to define structural anomalies.

#### 2. Thomas the Twin
* **Source Dialogue:** `parable_vineyard.json`
* **Identity:** A close follower of the Galilean movement, distinguished by a fiercely investigative mindset and a systemic refusal to accept simple allegories without inspecting their real-world consequences.
* **Social Status:** Marginalized by religious and political authorities, yet holds substantial internal, organic authority within the emerging underground movement as an honest interrogator.
* **Apparent Age:** Late 20s to mid-30s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Moderate. He is not currently in physical danger, but he is wrestling with the grim legal and physical fallout implied by the narratives he tracks.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Pragmatic, evidentiary, and plainspoken. He instantly translates allegorical folklore into hard judicial or civil realities (*"If you heard that in court, the men are guilty"*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Even, skeptical, yet deeply committed to truth. He uses words associated with testing and foundations: *questions, honest, court, guilty, stone, cornerstone*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He functions as a sounding board for the player's interpretive choices, explicitly asking them to strip away the spiritualized outer layers of a parable to confront its political or human core.

## 📜 Group 2: The Institutional Authorities
### Defensive, Reactive, Protectionist

#### 3. Simon the Pharisee
* **Source Dialogue:** `pharisee_critique.json`
* **Identity:** A localized civic scholar, synagogue elder, and community standard-bearer responsible for maintaining public moral boundaries and religious orthodoxy.
* **Social Status:** High local social standing. He is respected by the community but vulnerable to shifts in populist sentiment.
* **Apparent Age:** Mid-40s to early 50s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Elevated social and theological anxiety. The crowd’s emotional reaction to unauthorized, non-traditional healings directly threatens the civic stability he has spent his career building.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Formal, defensive, and structured. He deliberately attempts to distance himself from the lower classes by speaking of the populace in the detached third person (*"for some this town calls it conversation"*, *"the crowd needs an answer"*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Institutional, legalistic, and exclusionary. His speech is anchored by heavy nouns of tradition: *Synagogue, Law, Torah, Authoritative, Reconcile, Prophet*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He uses structural categorization as a defensive technique. He forces the player to reconcile immediate, disruptive human actions against timeless legal systems, using logic to dismantle emotional anomalies.

#### 4. The Temple Priest
* **Source Dialogue:** `priest_objection.json`
* **Identity:** An elite Sadducean or aristocratic temple functionary concerned with cosmic law, ritual order, and the preservation of the covenant made at Sinai.
* **Social Status:** Absolute institutional power. He outranks local Pharisees in wealth, political connection to the governor, and absolute religious authority.
* **Apparent Age:** 50s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** High systemic panic. A populist figure claiming to be "Lord of the Sabbath" is not a minor philosophical debate; it is an act of direct theological sedition.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Absolute, severe, and entirely non-negotiable. He relies on short, declarative sentences designed to shut down debate (*"The Law does not bend for crowds or healings"*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Grand, ancient, and historical. He uses sweeping labels of cosmic authority (*"Law of Sinai"*, *"Lord of the Sabbath"*) to minimize contemporary individual figures.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He offers the player rigid binary constraints. He demands that the conversation be brought back to institutional absolute laws, forcing a choice between total capitulation to the system or outright heresy.

## 🐓 Group 3: The Broken Inner Circle
### Traumatized, Visceral, Raw

#### 5. Simon Peter
* **Source Dialogues:** `peter_defense.json`, `peter_denial.json`
* **Identity:** The informal leader of the Galilean movement, living in the immediate aftermath of a public moral and psychological collapse.
* **Social Status:** Internally revered by his remaining peers, but completely compromised in his own mind; an outlaw to the state and a coward to himself.
* **Apparent Age:** Early 30s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Acute, haunting trauma and profound grief. He is speaking from a place of active emotional recovery.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Sudden, clipped, and completely unpolished. He lacks the sweeping rhetorical structure of the elite because he is speaking past his own shame. His sentences tumble out in a rapid-fire chronological sequence when recalling trauma (*"The rooster crowed. Then he looked. Then Peter left and wept..."*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Physical, immediate, and painfully raw. He uses active, unadorned verbs and concrete nouns: *healed, rooster, denied, wept, failure, mouth, words*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He provides raw emotional stakes. Dialogue choices with him do not revolve around dry legalities; they require navigating guilt, personal failure, and deciding whether his survival represents *"resilience or foolishness"*.

## ⚔️ Group 4: The Secular Powers
### Secular, Professional, Compartmentalized

#### 6. Claudius the Centurion
* **Source Dialogue:** `roman_assessment.json`
* **Identity:** A seasoned Roman military commander stationed at the Antonia Fortress, responsible for imperial peacekeeping and tactical security in a volatile province.
* **Social Status:** Dominant political authority as an officer of the empire, but a cultural outsider to local Judean domestic religion.
* **Apparent Age:** Late 30s to mid-40s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Stable but internally disrupted. While his physical environment is entirely secure, his professional paradigm has been challenged by an event that bypassed his military chain of command.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Crisp, logistical, and highly operational. He organizes thoughts like a military dispatch (*"I hold the Antonia fortress. I do not hold opinions..."*; *"before the messenger reported back"*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Transactional, orderly, and direct. He understands the universe through the mechanics of delegation and commands: *fortress, opinions, servant, paralyzed, messenger, faith, record*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He treats supernatural occurrences as transactional entries in a ledger. He challenges the player to view the narrative through a purely functional lens of power, authority, and execution.

#### 7. The Garrison Guard
* **Source Dialogue:** `guard_report.json`
* **Identity:** A working-class Roman auxiliary or conscripted guard stationed along the high outer walls of the Temple precinct during a public riot.
* **Social Status:** Low individual social status, but backed by the immediate, lethal weight of the Roman standard.
* **Apparent Age:** Early to mid-20s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Low. He views local ideological or religious conflicts with total cynical detachment. To him, public upheaval is not a battle for the cosmos; it is a shift-work inconvenience.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Colloquial, dismissive, and vividly observational. He relies on sensory snapshots to describe high-stakes historical flashpoints (*"tables overturned, animals scattered, the crowd orange with excitement"*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Flat, profane, and balancing grand terminology with administrative boredom (*"He called it My Father's house. The merchants called it their living. I called it my afternoon."*).
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He strips away historical romance. He forces the player to look at the material reality of a scene, serving as a reminder that the world outside the ideological bubble views the narrative with complete indifference.

## 🏺 Group 5: The Street & The Market
### Vulnerable, Cautious, Transactional

#### 8. The Market Informant
* **Source Dialogue:** `informant_bribe.json`
* **Identity:** A street-level opportunist, back-alley courier, or minor collaborator trading inside intelligence for physical safety and currency.
* **Social Status:** Extremely low and precarious. He is vulnerable to immediate violence from both political factions and institutional enforcers.
* **Apparent Age:** Variable (likely 30s).
* **Immediate Stress Level:** High. He is trading names in a heavily policed environment where exposure means immediate execution.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Symmetrical, repetitive, and cautious. He states parameters and establishes value before delivering information (*"Information has a price. Names have a price. Silence also has a price."*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Quantitative and predatory. He speaks in terms of accounts, transactions, and labels: *price, names, silence, ledger, paid, account*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He introduces immediate mechanical stakes. Dealing with him requires navigating asymmetric information economics, forcing the player to weigh financial cost against critical tactical clues (*"One of the twelve"*).

#### 9. The Nervous Citizen
* **Source Dialogue:** `rumor_whisper.json`
* **Identity:** An ordinary resident of Jerusalem caught in the dangerous crossfire of a regional religious and political crackdown.
* **Social Status:** Vulnerable lower-class citizen with zero systemic protection.
* **Apparent Age:** Indeterminate.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Acute fear of institutional retaliation. His physical movements dominate his linguistic pacing.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Fragmented, hushed, and reliant on literal quote attribution. He buffers himself from accusation by quoting the miracle recipient directly rather than stating it as personal belief (*"He said, 'I was blind. Now I can see.'"*).
  * **Vocabulary:** Basic, sensory, and defensive. He uses simple descriptive terms mixed with structural disclaimers: *glances, sideways, close, blind, walk, name, find, ask*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He passes the structural burden of investigation onto the player. He functions as an urgent, skittish quest-giver who drops raw witness locations while actively fleeing the dialogue scene (*"I will not say my name, but you should find him and ask."*).

#### 10. The Market Vendor
* **Source Dialogue:** `market_rumors.json`
* **Identity:** A local dry-goods or food merchant trying to leverage neighborhood gossip into increased stall foot traffic without drawing the eye of temple guards.
* **Social Status:** Stable working-class merchant.
* **Apparent Age:** 40s.
* **Immediate Stress Level:** Low to moderate. He values gossip as a commercial currency but remains alert to shifts in state surveillance.
* **Linguistic Style:**
  * **Syntax:** Welcoming, rhythmic, and conversational. He opens with an explicit hospitality greeting (*"Good day!"*) and immediate transitions to a trade context.
  * **Vocabulary:** Local, domestic, and light. He juxtaposes historical chaos with daily survival assets: *market, news, strange, stall, figs, rumours*.
* **Dialogue Tree Function:** He serves as an introductory dialogue node. He acts as a gatekeeper to deeper street gossip, requiring the player to declare their intentions (*"news, not the figs"*) before unlocking targeted regional rumors.
