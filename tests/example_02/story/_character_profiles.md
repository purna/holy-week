# Character Dialogue Profiles by Investigation Phase
## Systemic Script Parameters & Linguistic Casting

This reference manual structures all 36 narrative archetypes into the 12 distinct, linear investigation phases established in the dynamic player interface board.

---

## 📖 Global Systemic Overlays (Persistent)

#### 1. The Senior Scribe / Investigative Mentor
* **Source Dialogues:** [board_debate.json](board_debate.json), [board_review.json](board_review.json)
* **Phase Matrix:** Active across all 12 phases via the main investigation board interface.
* **Identity:** An aging Judean archival compiler monitoring the player.
* **Linguistic Style:** Highly academic and measured, arranging observations in structured triads.

## 🌴 ACT 1: THE POPULIST ARRIVAL

### Phase 1: The Donkey King
#### 2. The Galilean Pilgrim
* **Dialogue File:** [galilean_pilgrim.ink](galilean_pilgrim.ink)
* **Identity:** A rural traveler arriving for the festival who believes the new teacher is the promised peaceful deliverer.
* **Linguistic Style:** Pluralistic and collective language (*"we heard," "our village"*).

### Phase 2: Rumours in the Marketplace
#### 3. The Market Vendor
* **Source Dialogue:** [market_rumors.json](market_rumors.json)
* **Identity:** A street stall operator balancing hospitality with community chatter to increase customer foot traffic.
* **Linguistic Style:** Rhythmic, colloquial, and highly transactional.

#### 4. The Jerusalem Local
* **Dialogue File:** [jerusalem_local.ink](jerusalem_local.ink)
* **Identity:** A city dweller resentful of holiday price inflation and suspicious of rural Galilean fanaticism.
* **Linguistic Style:** Terse, direct, and laden with sharp urban idioms.

#### 5. Eleazar ben Simon (Sadducee Noble)
* **Dialogue File:** [eleazar_sadducee.ink](eleazar_sadducee.ink)
* **Identity:** A wealthy Sadducean aristocrat from Bethphage who filed a false deposition claiming the donkey was stolen, creating legal grounds for Roman intervention.
* **Linguistic Style:** Aristocratic, dismissive, and calculating — uses legal language to justify opposition.

#### 5b. The Woman with the Cloak
* **Dialogue File:** [woman_cloak.ink](woman_cloak.ink)
* **Identity:** A witness who touched the hem of the Galilean’s garment and was restored; she follows Him now at a distance.
* **Linguistic Style:** Reverent, quiet, and intensely personal.
